The 12-KPI Measurement Framework

How we audited creator performance, eliminated underperforming contracts, and optimized creator budgets.

01

Engagement Authenticity

Auditing comment-to-like ratios and account profiles to weed out bot spikes.

02

Audience Quality (AQI)

Analyzing follower demographics to verify alignment with our target buyers.

03

Sentiment Analysis

Scoring comment threads (Positive, Neutral, Negative) to assess brand impact.

04

Video Completion Rate

Measuring retention curves at 3s, 10s, and full length (67% completion vs 43% baseline).

05

Blended CPE

Tracking total cost per interactive action (likes, shares, saves, comments).

06

DM Lead Conversion

Measuring direct lead volume sourced through automated inbox replies.

07

Saves-to-Reach Ratio

Auditing content utility — high saves index indicates high future buyer intent.

08

SLA Response Rate

Sustaining under 24h response on 95% of inbound inquiries via CRM trackers.

09

Cost-per-Thousand (CPM)

Optimizing organic impression loops to lower media acquisition costs.

10

Referral Traffic Lift

Tracking organic link-in-bio clicks and coupon redemptions directly in Google Analytics 4.

11

Organic Virality Factor

Measuring friend-share multipliers on video reels vs static image cards.

12

Estimated ROI Ratio

Quarterly model correlating spend to revenue and custom pipeline growth metrics.
